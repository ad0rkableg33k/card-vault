🔑 Getting Your Free Google Gemini API Key

1
Go to Google AI Studio Visit aistudio.google.com/app/apikey — sign in with your Google account. No sign-up needed, just your existing Google login.
2
Click "Create API Key" Hit the blue Create API Key button. Choose Create API key in new project from the dropdown. It generates instantly.
3
Copy your key Your key looks like AIzaSy... — click the copy icon next to it. Save it somewhere safe like a notes app. You can always come back to this page to get it again.
4
Paste it into Card Vault Enter your key in the API Key field below — it turns green when valid. Your key stays in your browser only and is never stored or sent anywhere else.
🔒 Privacy: Your API key is saved to your browser's local storage so you don't have to re-enter it every visit. It never leaves your device — Card Vault has no backend server. You can clear it anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

💸 Cost: The free tier gives you 1,500 requests/day and 1 million tokens/minute — more than enough for even large collections. No billing required.
